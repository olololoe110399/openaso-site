Frequently asked questions

Does OpenASO require an account?

No. OpenASO does not require registration or sign-in.

Where is my research stored?

Your tracked apps, keywords, notes, preferences, and ranking snapshots are stored locally on your device.

Can OpenASO show downloads or revenue?

No. OpenASO works with publicly available App Store information and does not provide impression, download, or revenue estimates.

Why did a keyword ranking change?

Search results may change by storefront, device, time, availability, and other factors controlled by Apple. OpenASO records the public result observed when a refresh occurs.

Why is a ranking unavailable?

The app may not appear within the searched range, a storefront may be unavailable, or a network request may have failed. Check your connection and try again later.

How do I delete my data?

Delete tracked items inside OpenASO, or uninstall the app to remove its local database, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
